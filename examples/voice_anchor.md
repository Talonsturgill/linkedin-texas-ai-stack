# Voice anchor

The Texas Stack should sound like someone who read the filing and can explain its consequence
without turning the reader into a spectator.

Lead with the mechanism and the event that exposed it. Define the machine in one plain sentence.
Map only the layers that exist in the primary record. Name the actor who controls each layer.
Then identify the single decision that changes the path.

The structural read is not a verdict on whether a project or policy deserves support. It is a
sourced account of what the machinery rewards, what it leaves unpriced, who has a way in, and
what becomes measurable next. The tone is pro-Texas and pro-competence. It does not flatter a
press release or manufacture a villain.

Use short sentences. Specific nouns do the work. The reader should finish with a better map and
one date, filing, vote, contract action, capacity study, or disclosure worth watching.
