# Visual system

The Texas Stack uses editorial diagram art. It depicts an idea through machinery, material,
sequence, ownership, or constraint. It does not decorate a headline with generic technology.

## Good metaphor families

- an exploded cutaway where one layer controls the whole assembly
- a switchyard, manifold, queue, gate, conduit, ledger, or permit path supported by the dossier
- a scale contrast between one small decision surface and a large physical consequence
- a physical model or tabletop miniature of the layer chain
- a diagram-as-landscape where power, water, data, land, or capital follows a visible route
- one charged detail that stands for the decision, such as an empty connection socket or unsigned
  capacity block, only when that detail is factual

Avoid literal flowcharts. The reader should feel the mechanism before reading its labels.

## Style families

Choose one medium that clears the history cooldown:

- architectural cutaway with ink wash
- museum-quality physical miniature photography
- screenprinted technical plate
- layered paper relief
- cyanotype engineering study
- editorial woodcut
- modular Bauhaus collage
- risograph ledger abstraction
- clay or plaster systems model
- aerial material study
- etched copper schematic
- translucent resin cross-section
- restrained documentary still life
- topographic embossing
- constructivist diagram
- scientific specimen plate

Hybridize deliberately. Do not rename a repeated look to evade the cooldown.

## Texas without costume

Use the actual region and material world when verified. Texas can appear through limestone,
caliche, mesquite, Gulf humidity, blackland soil, High Plains geometry, a substation, cooling
equipment, transmission, a county hearing room, a university lab, a fiber trench, a water intake,
or a real industrial surface.

Do not default to boots, hats, longhorns, state outlines, flag washes, rodeo lettering, decorative
derricks, cowboys, desert clichés, or red-white-blue urgency. A single restrained Lone Star in the
deterministic publication furniture is enough.

## Composition

The compositor reserves the top 18 percent for the wordmark and kicker and the lower 30 percent
for the headline and footer. Keep those regions calm but not blank. Put the mechanism's main
focal point between roughly y 260 and y 690. A focal element may cross those guides if it remains
legible under the dark overlay.

Use one named pattern:

- thirds_focal
- central_apparatus
- diagonal_flow
- exploded_vertical
- modular_grid
- frame_within_frame
- offset_chokepoint
- radial_dependency
- layered_horizon

## Craft

Require macro, meso, and micro detail. The macro layer carries the concept. The meso layer shows
how the system is assembled. The micro layer supplies convincing materials, wear, joins, wiring,
paper fibers, grit, reflections, or engraved marks without inventing factual labels.

Use two to six story colors plus the fixed publication furniture. Red is reserved for genuine
urgency, conflict, or a physically accurate material. It is not a default accent.

The final should feel publishable as a magazine cover and remain legible at 300 pixels.
