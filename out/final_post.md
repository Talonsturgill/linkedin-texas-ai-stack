TCDC power-to-equity framework gives a power supplier a say in the next build.
New Era announced the deal on September 21st, 2026.

The framework pairs a power purchase contract with terms for cash support, project ownership and future power deals at an AI campus near Odessa.

- Supply. Luminant agrees to supply 200 to 207 MW. The filing allows power from the Odessa plant, other sources or the ERCOT grid. A nearby plant does not make this an exclusive physical supply path.
- Security. TCDC PowerCo must post a $116 million letter of credit within 15 business days of signing, plus up to $82.8 million in added security by delivery. These are required commitments, not proof that funds have been posted.
- Equity. New Era must arrange for Vistra to receive 5% non-voting equity after power starts flowing. That stake is in the project company for the portion supplied under the contract, not the whole parent company.
- Expansion rights. Vistra gets a right of first refusal on future onsite power build-out from April 2028. A separate first-offer right covers certain proposed power and battery projects. The two rights have different scopes.

The chokepoint is Vistra's choice to exercise or decline its right of first refusal on a covered future power opportunity. The public summary does not reveal the full process for that choice. It does not establish a veto over every campus decision.

The structure turns a power seller into a prospective owner with a place in later power deals. The non-voting stake and the expansion right do separate jobs. One shares in the project. The other gives its holder a choice when a covered opportunity arises.

For this Texas AI site, contracted supply is one step. Credit support and delivery conditions still stand between the announcement and power in use. New Era expects supply in 2027, but that is a forecast.

The next check is the September-quarter report, where New Era says it will file the full agreements. Read for the notice terms and limits on the first-refusal right, alongside evidence of posted security.
