NSF announced the Center for Human and Robot Co-Adaptation on August 26th, 2026.
The machinery behind it is an NSF Science and Technology Center cooperative agreement.

That vehicle funds a lead university in reviewed annual increments while NSF remains involved in a multi-institution research center.

- Selection layer. The assigned program officer recommends and the NSF Division Director acts on the award.
- Award authority layer. The NSF Grants and Agreements Officer signs the agreement and authorizes federal spending.
- Lead institution layer. The University of Texas at Austin holds the budget and oversees partner subawards.
- Center operations layer. Center Director Joydeep Biswas manages staffing and resource allocation across the center.
- Continuation layer. The principal investigator supplies the annual report that NSF uses to review continued support.

The headline number is not one unrestricted transfer. The official award record lists an estimated total of $29,999,998 and $5,999,999 obligated for fiscal year 2026. NSF describes the initial term as five years with roughly $6 million available each year. Support for each year remains contingent on a satisfactory review of progress and future plans.

The chokepoint sits in the Continuation layer. The principal investigator must submit the annual project report no later than 90 days before the current budget period ends. NSF says a missing report delays review and processing of future funding increments. The same reporting cycle feeds annual site visits and performance review.

This structure gives Austin real operating control without turning the full headline amount into a blank check. UT holds management and budget responsibility across six universities. Biswas controls staffing and resource allocation. The HERO Facility Network then moves the work into homes, hospitals, workplaces, and public spaces with support from the Texas Advanced Computing Center.

That is a strong vehicle for research that must change as evidence arrives. It also changes what Texans should count. The useful measure is not the five-year announcement alone. It is money obligated, performance reported, and another increment processed.

The next visible check is the award record. A new obligation would show that the mechanism advanced. UT can add substance by publishing the management indicators and site-level safeguards that informed the review.
