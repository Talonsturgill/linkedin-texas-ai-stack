Batch Zero Verification RFI process now stands between conditionally classified Large Loads and ERCOT's study queue.
ERCOT began issuing Batch Zero Verification Requests for Information on September 9, 2026.

The Batch Zero Verification RFI process is ERCOT's staged evidence review before a conditionally classified Large Load receives final Batch Zero eligibility.

- Pause authority layer starts with the PUCT good cause order that lets verification and audit finish before the study continues.
- Conditional classification layer applies ERCOT provisional classification and names the conditions attached to each submitted Large Load.
- Evidence and attestation layer runs through Interconnecting TSP contact designation, assigned questions, supporting documents, and sworn submissions in RIOO.
- Eligibility determination layer ends with ERCOT eligibility determination after review and any follow-up request for clarification.

The chokepoint is the Eligibility determination layer. ERCOT alone must determine eligibility for inclusion in Batch Zero. A complete response is an input, not the outcome. ERCOT can ask for more information, and a late or inadequate response excludes the Large Load from the process.

The response deadline makes the gate operational. The load entity, transmission provider, and any distribution provider must supply their assigned materials within 10 business days. The load entity may request no more than 5 additional business days. That limited relief does not move the decision away from ERCOT.

The structural effect is narrow but consequential. Several project-side actors assemble and attest to the record, while one grid operator controls whether the application advances. The process turns a provisional queue position into a claim that must survive evidence review. It also makes document completeness and response timing part of interconnection readiness rather than cleanup after the study begins.

For Texas, exclusion means the Large Load loses the transitional Batch Zero path and must wait for a future interconnection process. That consequence applies inside the ERCOT region, not every Texas grid.

The next visible check is ERCOT's Batch Zero Update scheduled for September 15, 2026. Watch whether ERCOT reports RFI issuance, completed responses, or eligibility outcomes. A second signal will be the separate Market Notice promised for the community-impact RFI.
