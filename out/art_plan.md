# TCDC power-to-equity framework cover blueprint

Prepared before generation on October 2nd, 2026. Headline is `Power comes with first refusal`.
Measured layout is out/art_layout.json. Illustration subject zone is [64, 196, 1016, 762] on a 1080 square.

Three concepts

1. Cable bookmark. An open, entirely blank contract book has a thick power cable used as a bookmark at the next page. The half-second read is that the power supplier has reserved a place in the next deal. The book and cable are declared metaphors for the PPA and future first-refusal right, not an actual contract or installation.
2. Split ceramic socket. A ceramic connector includes a small separable ownership segment and an unoccupied second connection. The half-second read is power paired with a project stake. Rejected because it privileges equity over the future decision and could imply an actual electrical specification.
3. First chair at the empty drafting table. A spare meeting table casts a large power-plug shadow from one occupied chair. The half-second read is the power supplier's place in future negotiations. Rejected because it risks implying a board seat, inconsistent with non-voting equity.

Selected concept is Cable bookmark. Its loose, removable placement expresses an option to exercise, not a locked barrier or general veto. The pages are blank and undated because full contract terms are not yet in the fetched record. No literal count or equipment layout is claimed.

Register and medium

Warm, tactile editorial woodcut with carved black strokes, cream paper forms and controlled ochre ink. Three-quarter overhead view of one open book, fanned right leaves, one insulated cable entering the spine area and resting between the first leaves, and a distinct plug end. This is a visual metaphor, not documentary evidence. No energized light, server building or completed campus appears.

Palette and roles

- #D5A52F warm ochre field and warm ink wash
- #24211B charcoal keyline and cable sheath
- #F5EDCF unprinted cream pages and highlights
- #9A7940 muted brown binding and carved recesses

Focal point, eye path and bounds

The cable pinched gently between the front leaves is the focal point, then the eye follows the cable to the plug and the unmarked fan of later pages. Thirds_focal composition. All story-bearing objects should fit inside [110, 235, 965, 715], well above the measured headline. Keep upper and lower regions quiet but opaque. The image must not imply the cable locks every page.

Detail at three scales

Macro is a clear book silhouette and unmistakable power plug. Meso is the opening between the first leaves and the cable resting there, plus a short visible cable curve. Micro is carved crosshatching, irregular print pressure, page fibers and cable ribbing. No labels, symbols or invented numbers.

Risks and repair plan

Reject a padlock, chain, hand, boardroom seat, illegible pseudo-type, live power glow or equipment diagram. Reject cable placement that blocks all pages or fails to read as bookmarking a next deal. Inspect full resolution and 300 pixels before scoring. Measure actual object bounds before composition. Repair one concrete defect at a time, retain rejected generations and scores, and require weighted score at least 8.5 with no dimension under 7 and no known blockers.
