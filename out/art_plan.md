# Pre-production blueprint

Prepared September 25th, 2026 before the first ImageGen call. Mechanism and post are final. The subject is the TCEQ permit hold pending audit review, depicted as a conceptual assembly rather than a real facility or agency document.

## Three concepts

1. **The held permit ribbon.** A broad ivory paper ribbon stops against one closed pleated shutter. Two thinner blue and ochre paper paths approach the shutter from above as water and grid evidence. Half-second read is a permit path held at a single decision surface.
2. **The suspended approval leaf.** An unmarked sheet hangs between separate water and energy evidence layers. Half-second read is permission awaiting support. Rejected because suspended layers echo the prior funding-wafer cover and make the controller less clear.
3. **The unfinished audit bridge.** An abstract path across a narrow gap remains missing its final segment. Half-second read is incomplete evidence preventing passage. Rejected because a missing physical bridge could imply infrastructure failure absent from the sources.

## Selection and register

Select the held permit ribbon. One closed surface ties the image directly to issuance being withheld. Blue and ochre are abstract evidence paths, not actual pipelines, transmission routes, or claims about a named campus. Use patient tension and precision, without alarm, partisanship, victory or blame. Headline is THE AUDIT NOW HOLDS THE PERMIT. Place is TEXAS. Category is REGULATORY.

## Detail scales and risks

Macro is one broad interrupted path. Meso is folds, layered shutter construction and two distinct evidence strips. Micro is exposed paper fibers, subtle cut edges, compressed fold ridges and fine contact shadows. No incidental labels, writing, printed paper, numbering, icons or seals.

Reject a visually open gate, a ribbon that passes through the shutter, flat featureless card, generic circuitry, tiny objects, an overloaded dark background, or a subject that collides with the headline. Do not portray a real map, office, data center, power plant, reservoir, person or exact physical system. The graphic is an editorial metaphor, not a technical schematic.
