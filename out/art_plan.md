# The Texas Stack artwork blueprint

## Three concepts

### 1. The verification manifold

Mechanism-specific metaphor: four separate evidence channels feed a compact inspection chamber, but a single heavy gate arm at the chamber's exit controls whether the final load block reaches the Batch Zero rail.

Half-second read: many inputs, one eligibility gate.

### 2. The provisional socket

Mechanism-specific metaphor: a large industrial connector waits one deliberate step short of a bus rail while four verified material layers align behind it. The small open socket represents conditional rather than final inclusion.

Half-second read: connected on paper, not yet cleared.

### 3. The attestation press

Mechanism-specific metaphor: four blank, fiber-rich submission sheets enter a precise mechanical press whose single platen compresses them into one dense eligibility tile. A side chute shows the consequence of a failed fit without depicting a real project.

Half-second read: a distributed record becomes one binary outcome.

## Selected concept

The verification manifold is truer than the alternatives because it preserves both halves of the mechanism. Several named parties assemble separate parts of the response, while ERCOT controls the single eligibility determination at the exit. The provisional socket overweights the result, and the attestation press overweights documents at the expense of routing and review.

## Emotional register

Calm procedural tension. The machinery should feel consequential and exact, not alarmist, celebratory, or punitive.

## Style and cooldown proof

Style family: museum-quality physical miniature photography with restrained documentary still-life lighting.

This clears the history cooldown. It is not the recent translucent resin cross-section, generic generated editorial art, or deterministic dusk fallback. The selected motif is a grounded four-channel inspection manifold, not suspended funding wafers. The offset chokepoint composition is distinct from the recent exploded vertical and generic full-bleed focal field. The graphite, limestone, oxidized copper, and mesquite hue family is not the recent indigo-gold or Big Bend dusk family.

## Palette

- `#0F0C1C` deep graphite for the miniature base and quiet publication bands
- `#EDE6D6` limestone for the four evidence channels and legible material edges
- `#8C6A4A` oxidized copper for the inspection chamber and gate hardware
- `#6E7A5A` mesquite green for the cleared exit rail and restrained directional cue
- `#55606F` blue steel for joins, fasteners, and secondary mechanical detail

## Coordinate map

- Canvas: 1080 by 1080
- Quiet top band: x 0 to 1080, y 0 to 194, dark textured wall with no focal detail
- Focal point: x 650, y 480, the compact inspection chamber and one gate arm
- Input channels: four shallow tracks begin near x 150, y 300 through 650 and converge from upper left toward the focal chamber
- Cleared exit rail: x 690 to 940, y 500 to 585, short and visually subordinate to the gate
- Quiet lower headline band: x 0 to 1080, y 756 to 1080, dark work surface with low-contrast material grain only
- Eye path: upper-left parallel channels, inward convergence, copper chamber, single gate arm, short mesquite exit

Composition name: offset_chokepoint.

## Detail hierarchy

- Macro: four separate channels converge on one inspection gate
- Meso: channel joins, document-like blank inserts, a compact review chamber, a single pivoting gate arm, and one exit rail
- Micro: limestone fibers, worn blue-steel fasteners, oxidized copper edges, shallow dust, restrained reflections, fine joins, and credible miniature scale

## Risks and controls

- Visual cliché: avoid glowing circuitry, server aisles, brains, digital locks, and literal flowchart arrows
- False geography: include no map, state outline, skyline, terrain claim, or project-specific location
- False document claim: submission inserts remain blank with no seals, signatures, stamps, numbers, or generated writing
- Weak hierarchy: keep the single copper gate arm brighter and sharper than the input tracks
- Type collision: preserve the top 18 percent and lower 30 percent as calm, dark, low-detail regions
- Punitive tone: show a neutral inspection gate rather than a barricade, broken equipment, warning lights, or rejection stamp
