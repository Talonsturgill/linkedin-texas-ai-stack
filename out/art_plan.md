# Texas Stack artwork blueprint, October 9th, 2026

The selected subject is the Taylor cooling-water exception framework. This is conceptual editorial art of the temporary-emergency permission route, not a drawing of installed pipes, the actual campus or all potable-water exceptions.

## Three concepts

1. A porcelain valve with a fountain-pen nib as its handle. The half-second read is that writing controls this water route. The city expressly requires written City Manager approval for temporary emergencies. A clay systems model gives that administrative step physical weight.
2. A documentary still life of a blank approval sheet reflected in a glass of water. The half-second read is a decision with a household resource at stake. It is restrained but the permission mechanism is less legible without a caption, and the previous book/contract motif is too close.
3. An ink-wash water loop with a small empty inlet shaped by negative space. The half-second read is a conditional second supply. It risks suggesting an actual engineering arrangement and hiding the written decision, so it is rejected.

Selected concept is the porcelain pen-valve. Its one impossible element is an explicit metaphor for written permission, not fabricated infrastructure. No real seals, signatures, flow arrows, campus outline or technical labels appear. The valve is one emergency route and does not imply that every listed exception needs the same approval.

## Direction

Register is curious, precise and calm. Medium is a visibly hand-built clay or plaster systems model with glazed ceramic surfaces, not photographic miniature architecture. Composition is central_apparatus. A large ivory valve sits horizontally with a cobalt fountain-pen nib rising from its spindle. A short pipe segment ends in a suspended cobalt water drop. There is no open real-world tap or operating campus claim.

Palette roles are cobalt #254EDB for the backdrop and nib, milk porcelain #F0F1E8 for the pipe, midnight blue #101D4C for depth, pale lime #DCE9A4 for a restrained reflected edge, and cool gray #A8B4CB for shaded ceramic. Hue family is cobalt-porcelain-lime.

Focal point is the nib-to-spindle join. The eye moves from nib to valve body to the water drop, then to the headline. Macro detail is the strong valve silhouette. Meso detail is the clay flange seam and rotating spindle collar. Micro detail is ceramic glaze pooling, softly irregular hand-worked edges and a clean nib slit. No decorative mechanical clutter.

Headline is THE EMERGENCY WATER DECISION. Measured bounds live in art_layout.json and are copied exactly into the generation prompt before rendering. Every story-bearing object stays inside that subject zone, with spare clearance. Lower field remains empty for type. Lighting is one broad upper-left light, soft shadow to lower right, no glare that burns out the nib.
