# Pre-production blueprint

Story boundary: PGRR144 proposes a comparison between the electrical model used in a stability study and the computing facility as built. ERCOT would approve the submission or ask for more data. PUCT approval is pending. Show a symbolic comparison, not a real facility, approved connection, live grid or government endorsement.

## Three distinct concepts

1. **Registration frame**. A richly engraved computing-and-cooling block and its pale line-drawn counterpart sit on two planes that meet inside one exacting inspection frame. Half-second read: the machine must meet its model. A screenprinted technical plate, not a literal schematic.
2. **Twin impressions**. Two embossed paper impressions of the same mechanical form almost align under a raking light, with one small gap. Half-second read: study and installed form must agree. Layered paper relief. Risks suggesting a known real-world mismatch that has not been documented.
3. **Unclosed lens**. A large inspection lens brings a model and an equipment block into the same focal plane. Half-second read: evidence remains under examination. Editorial woodcut. Risks generic inspection imagery and a third object distracting from the comparison.

## Selected concept

Registration frame is truest to the specific before-and-after model comparison. The frame stands for ERCOT review but bears no seal, stamp, tick mark, success light or implied approval. The two views are related but are not a claim that a particular facility's data is wrong. This is an editorial metaphor, not a technical drawing or map.

Emotional register: exacting, patient, tangible. The interest lies in careful correspondence rather than alarm.

Style family: screenprinted technical plate, with fine engraved hatching and subtle ink texture. History prohibits museum miniature photography, translucent resin cross-section, generic story-specific generated editorial art and dusk fallback; none is used.

Primary motif: paired machine-and-model registration frame. History prohibits four evidence channels, five suspended funding wafers and a generic story-specific focal metaphor. The selected specific comparative apparatus is new.

Composition: frame_within_frame. History prohibits offset_chokepoint and exploded_vertical. No vertical stack, manifold or queue is used.

Hue family: petrol-mint-chalk. History prohibits graphite-limestone-copper-mesquite, indigo-gold and dusk furniture as the main hue family.

## Risks and remedies

Avoid a generic circuit-board background, science-fiction portal or actual electrical one-line diagram. Keep the frame incomplete as a neutral review aperture, not a lock indicating a failure. Do not depict a closed switch or approved energized facility. No geography, specific county, invented site, brand, authority seal, number, word, person, flag or political imagery. Preserve large readable silhouette at 300 pixels and enough quiet surface for exact type.
