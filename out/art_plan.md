# Art plan: 2026-10-10 no-target cover

## Selected concept

- Render family: editorial_scene. Style family: sculptural editorial still life.
- Story treatment: incomplete_evidence. Motif: missing keystone.
- Composition: opposed_banks. Hue family: moss_magenta.
- Seed 962876846. Dossier SHA-256 59d131fab63133b7ef7c8fcd3fe495f246e66c14867c40a270c7a721764e18e1.

## Selection rationale

No mechanism cleared the 21-day gate, so the honest visual claim is an incomplete evidence chain,
not a project, an approval or a piece of equipment. Two arch halves on plinths with the keystone
absent show a chain that does not close. The image says "unfinished support" and nothing about a
specific actor, permit or facility.

Two other concepts were generated for the blueprint: a charcoal split field with a sliding pin, and
a volume ascending-terrace study with a locking collar. Both are catalog patterns with no direct
link to a missing link in the record, so they were not chosen.

## Literal dossier bindings

- Binding name: "Incomplete source chain" (no_target, no layers).
- Decision: "Withhold publication until the evidence chain is complete".
- Controller field: "Editorial verification". This is the planner's no-target binding, not a
  regulator, agency or decision-maker outside this repository.

## Metaphor limit

Editorial metaphor, not an engineering drawing. No labels, maps, seals, signatures, project names,
equipment layouts or approval marks are drawn.

## Medium and palette roles

- Medium: sculptural editorial still life, opaque studio render, no text in the base.
- Dark ground: #0B1309. Deep shadow: #192515.
- Main body: #87A655 (moss). Light edge: #E5E8C9 (cream).
- Accent at the joins and plinths: #DD6396 (magenta), used sparingly.

## Focal point and eye path

- Focal point: the gap at the center where the keystone is absent.
- Eye path: the two arch halves, then the gap, then the plinths, then the headline below.
- Quiet zone: the lower band is reserved for the headline by art_layout.json.

## Detail at three scales

- Thumbnail (about 300 px): two large arch silhouettes with a clear central gap.
- Medium: plinth grounding, cast shadows and the magenta joint accent.
- Full size: bevel highlights on the cream edge and the shading along each joint.

## Visual risks

- The arch could read as ordinary architecture rather than evidence. The gap and the plinths
  carry the metaphor, but this is the main risk.
- Magenta accent could compete with the focal gap. Keep it to the joins only.
- Headline lines must not overlap the arch feet; layout reserves rows from y=740.
